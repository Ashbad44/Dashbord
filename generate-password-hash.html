Generate a password hash

This runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded or sent anywhere, it works even without internet. Open this file by double-clicking it on your computer.

Pick a password that's long and not guessable (a short phrase like Blue-Badr-Roof-42 is much stronger than a single word or a birthdate).

Values will appear here…

Copy both values into config.js:
SITE_PASSWORD_SALT and SITE_PASSWORD_HASH.
Do NOT put your plain password into config.js — only these two values. Each time you click "Generate hash" you get a new random salt, so re-run this whenever you change your password.
